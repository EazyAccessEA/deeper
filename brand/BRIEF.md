# deeper: brand brief

## Positioning

deeper is a free skill that makes your AI agent ask for a task the way the best person in that field would, then waits for your go-ahead.

## Audience

Developers and makers who use coding agents every day. They read READMEs on GitHub, distrust AI hype, and decide in seconds whether a tool is serious. Many are designers or writers too, not only engineers.

## Brand idea: the expert's mark-up

deeper takes your request and marks it up the way an editor marks up a manuscript. It inserts what an expert would add (the raw material, the instrument, the check) and leaves your scope alone.

The visual language comes from proofreading, not from computers:

- **The caret (^)**, the proofreader's sign for "insert here". It's the mark. deeper's whole job is inserting what was missing.
- **One annotation colour**, like an editor's pencil, used only for what deeper adds. Everything else stays in plain ink.
- **Your words and the expert's additions, side by side.** The demo shows the plain request with the insertions marked, before the full prompt.

## Voice

Plain, specific, a little dry. Says what something does. No hype words, no invented numbers, no claims we can't show.

## It must never look like

1. A terminal or blueprint: monospace labels everywhere, hairline grids, fake telemetry readouts, depth gauges. (The last version.)
2. AI SaaS: purple or blue gradients, glowing cards, three equal feature boxes.
3. Cream-paper editorial: warm beige, a high-contrast serif and a terracotta accent.

## Assets needed

Mark and wordmark, favicon (SVG, 16, 32), apple-touch icon (180), GitHub social preview (1280x640), Open Graph card (1200x630), README header, a one-page brand board.

## Tests

- The mark reads at 16 px and in one colour.
- Five-second test with 3 to 5 developers: they can say what it is, and pick our hero out of a line-up of four AI-made developer-tool pages. Pass is 3 of 5.
