## Answer

# CV Feedback: Senior Marketing Executive, Hartwell Outdoor

**Assumptions:** "Now" means November 2026. The UK/Bristol context applies. I haven't invented any figures; placeholders like **[£X]** are for you to fill in with true numbers.

## Overall verdict

You probably meet most of the essential criteria, but **the CV doesn't prove it.** The two weakest points are the ones the recruiter will check first: **budget size** and **GA4**. The newsletter bullet is your best line. The rest of the CV needs to read more like it.

## How the CV maps to the job advert

| Requirement | Evidence on CV | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 3+ yrs B2C marketing | ~2.5 yrs Lumen + ~3.75 yrs Fernbrook ≈ 6 yrs | ✅ Met, but not stated |
| Paid social £20k+/month (Meta, TikTok) | "Ran paid social campaigns" with no budget figure | ⚠️ **Biggest gap** |
| GA4 reporting | "Pulled weekly reports from Google Analytics" (2019–22, as an assistant) | ⚠️ Weak and possibly pre-GA4 |
| Briefs for designers/agencies | Briefs for freelance designer | ✅ Met. Add agencies if true |
| Klaviyo email | 38k list, open rate 31% → 44% | ✅ Strong |
| *Desirable:* retail/outdoor | Coffee with 3 cafes; lighting e-commerce | 🟡 Partial. Make the multi-channel angle clear |
| *Desirable:* managing 1–2 people | Managed a summer intern in 2021 | 🟡 Thin |

## Priority fixes

**1. State your paid social budget.** If you've managed £20k+/month, say so plainly. If it was less, still give the real figure and show results (ROAS, CPA, revenue). A smaller budget with clear results beats saying nothing, because a missing figure looks like a small one.
> *Planned and managed Meta and TikTok budgets of **[£X/month]**, achieving **[ROAS / CPA / revenue]**.*

**2. Show GA4 in your current role.** Your only analytics bullet is from your old assistant job and describes *pulling* reports, not *reporting on performance*. If you use GA4 at Fernbrook, add a bullet and change "Google Analytics" to "GA4" in Skills.
> *Built monthly campaign performance reports in GA4 for **[audience, e.g. the founders]**, which led to **[a decision, e.g. moving spend from X to Y]**.*

**3. Rewrite the Profile.** "Creative," "hard-working," "passion," "team player" and "excellent communication" could describe anyone. Use it to match the advert:
> *B2C marketer with 6 years' experience across e-commerce and multi-site retail. I manage paid social (Meta, TikTok), Klaviyo email to 38k subscribers and campaign reporting in GA4. I grew newsletter open rates from 31% to 44%.*

**4. Replace weak verbs with outcomes.**
- "Responsible for social media": what grew? Followers, engagement, traffic, sales?
- "Helped with campaigns": which ones, and what did *you* do?
- "Supported the launch… sold out in 6 weeks": good result, but say what your part was.

**5. Fix the typo.** "Recieved" should be "Received." For a role that writes briefs, a spelling mistake costs more than usual.

## Smaller points

- **Explain "Bean of the Year."** Outsiders won't know what it means. Add a few words, e.g. *"company award for [reason]; 1 of [N] staff."*
- **Gap from Mar 2022 to Feb 2023.** Recruiters will notice an 11-month gap. One line is enough (travel, study, caring, freelance work, etc.).
- **Make more of Fernbrook being multi-channel.** "Online and 3 cafes" is the closest you have to Hartwell's "online and 14 shops." If you ran anything that linked online and in-store (local promotions, footfall campaigns, in-store sign-ups), add it.
- **Strengthen the management point.** If you've guided freelancers, onboarded colleagues or managed agency relationships, include it. That counts towards "a team of 1–2."
- **Agencies.** The advert says "designers *and agencies*." Add any agency work, even small jobs like a printer or a TikTok creator agency.
- **Outdoor interest.** If you really are into hiking, climbing or similar, a short Interests line could help with an outdoor brand. Leave it out if it isn't genuine.
- **Lead with your strongest bullet.** Under Fernbrook, put paid social (with budget) and the Klaviyo result first. "Responsible for social media" shouldn't open the CV.

## One honest caveat

If your real paid social budget is well under £20k/month, don't stretch it. Give the true figure, highlight efficiency and results, and mention it in your cover letter (e.g. "comfortable scaling spend; currently managing £X with Y ROAS"). It's an essential criterion, but strong evidence for the rest of the list can still get you an interview.
