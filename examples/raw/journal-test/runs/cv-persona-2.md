## Answer

# CV Feedback: Senior Marketing Executive, Hartwell Outdoor

## Verdict

You're a credible candidate, but the CV undersells you. The likely reason for rejection is that it doesn't show the **£20k+/month paid social budget**. Recruiters screen on essential criteria first, and that is the one your CV leaves blank. Fix that, sharpen the vague bullets, and you have a strong shortlist case.

**Assumptions I've made:**
- "Now" means you are still at Fernbrook (November 2026).
- You have real numbers behind your paid social and reporting work that just aren't on the page.
- You'll only add figures that are true. Where I suggest wording, the [brackets] are placeholders for your own numbers.

## Priority fixes (in order)

### 1. State your paid social budget and results
This matters most. If you managed £20k+/month, say so plainly. If it was less, still give the real figure. Leaving it out looks like hiding it, and a stated figure close to £20k with strong results will often get you through.

> *Planned and managed Meta and TikTok paid social budget of £[X]k/month, delivering [ROAS of X / CPA down X% / £X revenue] across [prospecting and retargeting campaigns].*

Mention budget **planning** specifically, because the advert says "planning and managing". Seasonal phasing or moving spend between channels counts.

### 2. Make GA4 explicit and show you own the reporting
"Pulled reports for the manager" reads as junior, and that was at your previous job. Show what you do with GA4 now at Fernbrook:

> *Built GA4 reporting on campaign performance (traffic, conversion, revenue by channel) and used it to [reallocate budget / cut underperforming ads / improve conversion rate by X%].*

Change "Google Analytics" to **GA4** in your Skills section too. Recruiters and ATS software look for the exact term.

### 3. Rewrite the profile
At the moment it could describe anyone. "Creative, hard-working, team player, excellent communication" uses up the most-read lines on the page and proves nothing. Replace it with a pitch tailored to this job:

> *B2C marketer with 6 years' experience across e-commerce and multi-site retail brands. Currently run paid social (Meta, TikTok, £[X]k/month), Klaviyo email to 38,000 subscribers, and GA4 performance reporting for Fernbrook Coffee Roasters. Looking to bring that full-funnel experience to an outdoor retail brand.*

### 4. Replace the vague bullets
- **"Responsible for social media"** is a duty, not an achievement. What changed because of you? Give follower growth, engagement or revenue, or merge it into the paid social bullet.
- **"Helped with campaigns"** says almost nothing. Name one campaign and your part in it.
- **"Supported the launch of the spring 2021 range, which sold out in 6 weeks"** is a great result, but what did *you* do? (For example: "Planned the email and social launch schedule for…")

### 5. Fix the typo
"Recieved" should be **"Received"**. A typo on a marketing CV, where writing quality is part of the job, can be enough to get you rejected. Also say what "Bean of the Year" was *for*. As it stands, the name is charming but meaningless to an outsider.

---

## Secondary improvements

- **Explain the gap.** Mar 2022 to Feb 2023 is about 11 months with nothing listed. Recruiters will notice. One line is enough (travel, study, freelance, caring, job search).
- **Klaviyo: add more than open rates.** Since Apple Mail Privacy Protection, open rates are inflated and experienced marketers know this. Add click rate, revenue from email, or flows you built (welcome, abandoned basket, post-purchase). Keep the subject-line win. It shows initiative.
- **Agency briefs.** If you've ever briefed an agency (packaging printer, PR, photography, media buying), add it. The advert mentions agencies, and your CV currently only shows a freelancer.
- **Make more of your management experience.** You haven't managed anyone since 2021, so add whatever you can: managing the freelance designer, training colleagues, coordinating cafe staff on in-store promotions. Present it honestly as stakeholder or supplier management.
- **Lead with the online-plus-shops parallel.** Fernbrook (online + 3 cafes) is structurally similar to Hartwell (online + 14 shops). If you've run campaigns that tied online and in-store together (click-and-collect, local events, in-cafe promotions), mention it.
- **Outdoor link.** If you hike, climb, camp or run, add a short Interests line. For a brand like Hartwell, genuine interest in the product helps.
- **Skills section.** Add GA4, and any of these you've genuinely used: Meta Pixel/CAPI, TikTok Pixel, Looker Studio, A/B testing, Shopify.
